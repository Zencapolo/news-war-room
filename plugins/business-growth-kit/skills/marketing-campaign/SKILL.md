# Marketing Campaign

Use this skill when the user asks for campaign plans, launch plans, social content, email sequences, ad angles, content calendars, audience messaging, or marketing experiments.

## Workflow

1. Determine the campaign brief:
   - Business goal
   - Product, service, or offer
   - Target audience
   - Core pain or desire
   - Channel mix
   - Timeframe
   - Budget or resource constraints
   - Brand voice and claims restrictions

2. If details are missing, make practical assumptions and list them briefly.

3. Design the campaign:
   - Campaign thesis
   - Audience segments
   - Core message and proof points
   - Offer or call to action
   - Channel plan
   - Content themes
   - Calendar
   - Measurement plan
   - Risks and experiments

4. Adapt output to the requested channel:
   - LinkedIn: professional, specific, discussion-worthy
   - Email: short subject lines, clear value, one primary action
   - Ads: sharp hook, benefit, proof, CTA
   - Blog or SEO: search intent, structure, internal linking ideas
   - Short video: visual hook, spoken script, scene beats

## Output Style

Make campaign plans immediately usable. Prefer concrete examples over abstract advice. For content calendars, include dates or week labels, channel, asset type, topic, hook, CTA, and owner placeholder when useful.

## Quality Bar

- Tie every tactic to a business goal.
- Avoid generic inspirational language.
- Include a measurement approach.
- Respect brand, compliance, and claims limits.
- Include variations that can be tested.
