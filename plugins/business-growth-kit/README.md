# Business Growth Kit

Business Growth Kit is a starter Codex plugin for common commercial workflows. It is intentionally generic so it can be tested immediately, then customized with company-specific positioning, voice, pricing logic, and document formats.

## Included Skills

- `proposal-writer`: Turns a business opportunity into a structured sales proposal, outreach email, and follow-up plan.
- `competitor-brief`: Compares competitors and produces positioning, risk, and sales battlecard notes.
- `marketing-campaign`: Builds campaign concepts, channel plans, content angles, and measurement ideas.

## Suggested Test Prompts

```text
Use proposal-writer to create a proposal for a logistics company that wants an AI customer support assistant.
```

```text
Use competitor-brief to compare our CRM product against HubSpot, Salesforce, and Zoho for small B2B teams.
```

```text
Use marketing-campaign to plan a four-week launch campaign for a new accounting automation tool.
```

## Customization Ideas

- Add brand voice rules and banned phrases.
- Add preferred proposal sections and pricing assumptions.
- Add competitor lists by market segment.
- Add company case studies, proof points, and approved claims.
